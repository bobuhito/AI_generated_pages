How it works

A closed path is sampled as complex numbers zn. The discrete Fourier transform yields coefficients ck:

ck = (1/N) Σ zn e−2πi k n / N

Each ck is a rotating vector with frequency k. Sorted by amplitude and summed, they reconstruct the original shape. More terms → finer detail.
